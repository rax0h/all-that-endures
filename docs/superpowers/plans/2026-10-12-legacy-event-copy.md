Goal: explicitly normalize raw legacy Event payload graphs while retaining
captured incarnation IDs, values/order/sharing, source bytes and held overlays.

Build new container/record backing changes with existing constructors/headers in
the unpublished mutable-empty destination. Existing checked legacy occurrence
labels identify mutable children. Replace ancestor-owned deep paths with actual
physical page/entry/record-field placements. Rewrite all affected copied aliases,

they have no legacy mutable identity labels. No ordinary migration or format6 claim.

agreement and cycles before publication. Event-ID authority references and raw
container aliases that would replace a concrete family root require their existing
special authority integration; reject unresolved cases rather than split aliases.
