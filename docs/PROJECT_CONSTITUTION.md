# Project Constitution

## Product definition

**All That Endures** is a 2.75D fantasy RPG set in a procedurally generated, single-continent world whose history is simulated before the player enters it. The same world remains causally active during play.

The world is not a backdrop for generated quests. Geography, people, institutions, objects, ecology, magic, memory and culture are persistent state connected by causality.

> **The interaction graph is the product.**

## World contract

1. A **World Seed** creates the physical world first: continent, coastlines, elevation, geology, watersheds, climate, biomes, resources and ecological starting conditions.
2. Initial populations and societies are then seeded into that physical reality.
3. Long-horizon history runs from those conditions without knowledge of where the player will begin or travel.
4. The entire named map is part of the simulated world and is theoretically playable.
5. The world is intentionally game-sized rather than required to match Earth-scale astronomy. It must be large enough for geography, travel, cultural separation and exploration to matter.
6. Generated history may name only places grounded in the simulated world. Claims about nonexistent or mythical places may exist as beliefs, but the belief itself must have provenance.
7. A world is reproducible from its seeds plus simulation/version contracts.

## Causality constitution

- **No system may fabricate historical explanation when causal provenance exists.**
- **No quest, legend, rumor, historical claim, environmental story or NPC account may create objective history. It may only interpret, distort, conceal, discover or respond to history that actually exists.**
- **Player relevance may determine simulation resolution, but it may never determine historical probability.**
- **Narrative systems may search Reality for meaningful pressures; they may not alter Reality to optimize entertainment.**
- **The simulation must generate people before it generates content attributed to people.**
- If the same event repeatedly produces the same kind of person, response or story, the model is too shallow.
- If wildly different outcomes occur without traceable reasons, the model is too random.

Every meaningful historical entity or event must be able to answer, directly or through compressed causal structure: **What caused me? What did I cause?**

## Reality, Society, Knowledge, Narrative

**Reality** is what physically and causally happened.

**Society** is how intelligent beings organize and respond: relationships, families, organizations, law, politics, markets, culture and institutions.

**Knowledge** is what observers, memories, records, scholarship and traditions claim about Reality. Knowledge may be incomplete, contradictory or wrong.

**Narrative** exposes unresolved pressures, motives, mysteries and opportunities already present in the other layers. Narrative is not an alternate history generator.

## Player contract

The player is an inhabitant, not a privileged causal exception. Relationships, crimes, businesses, discoveries, possessions, organizations, descendants, battles, rank advancement and death use the same world rules available to NPCs. If the player becomes historically important, later memory and scholarship must emerge from what actually occurred.

Experiential acceptance test:

> **Walk back into a settlement years later and recognize what happened.**

## Rank ontology

Rank is not a cosmetic power level. Advancement progressively changes what kind of organism a person is. Rank consequences must be centralized and explicit across biology, metabolism, sustenance, aging, cognition, perception, movement, healing, injury, disease, reproduction, appearance, sleep, temperature, respiration, magical needs, labor, combat, travel, economics, medicine, law, culture and psychology.

Current direction to preserve while exact thresholds remain to be canonized:

- Most people possess little or no magic. Many have no Essence; some have limited Essence/skill access.
- Cognitive capability can change with rank without homogenizing personality, creativity or judgment.
- Ordinary food becomes less biologically necessary at higher ranks; magical sustenance/spirit coins become increasingly important.
- At sufficiently high rank, ordinary organs can cease to be required. A body may become magically organized living tissue maintaining a human form without conventional anatomy.
- Rank must therefore change appearance, injury semantics, medicine, survival needs and social/economic behavior, not merely stats or visual effects.
- Exact thresholds and mechanics are **unresolved canon** until explicitly defined.

## Art constitution

The presentation target is premium illustrated fantasy realism built on a genuinely spatial 3D world. Terrain, elevation, occlusion, architecture, navigation, shadows, water, vegetation, particles, weather and physically situated magic are real runtime systems. The camera is controlled, elevated and forward-looking in a three-quarter presentation.

The goal is not unrestricted photorealism. It is to spend production quality on the pixels the controlled camera actually presents and produce frames with the composition, material richness, atmosphere and beauty of high-end fantasy illustration.

Procedural variation is never permission to lower the art standard.

## Asset constitution

The game authors a finite, exceptional visual vocabulary and composes it through constrained grammars. We author atoms and rules, not every possible world state.

- Major spatial structures generally use true geometry/materials.
- Characters use persistent parametric identity driven by genetics, age, life history, culture, wealth, occupation, injury, rank and Essence influence.
- Cultural visual traditions have lineage and may hybridize through real contact, migration, conquest, teaching and imitation.
- Buildings preserve construction history and visually realize additions, damage, repairs, ownership and changing style.
- Transparent runtime assets are authored with genuine transparency from generation/source time. Background removal is not a normal production step.
- Source masters and optimized runtime derivatives remain distinct.

## First proof

The first implementation proof is the **Visual World Laboratory**. It must demonstrate that the actual architecture can generate many visually convincing, historically changing settlement states from simulation-derived specifications. One hand-authored hero screenshot is not proof.
