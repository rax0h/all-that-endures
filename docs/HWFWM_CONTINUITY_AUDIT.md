# HWFWM continuity audit for All That Endures

This is a design audit, not a mandate to copy story-specific names or plot. It identifies systemic continuity lessons from publicly documented HWFWM lore that ATE should reproduce in its own setting.

## Advancement

- Iron rank is the beginning of a complete essence configuration; Bronze requires all abilities to have advanced to Bronze.
- Active adventurers can plausibly reach Bronze on a career timescale measured in a few years, not decades. Regional training quality matters: poor training creates stalled Iron populations while serious adventurers leave weak regions for better opportunities.
- Combat is not the only advancement path. Utility/craft abilities can advance through meaningful limits and imposed consequences; monster cores are a faster alternative and preferentially help lagging abilities.
- Core-heavy advancement should carry a later cost: Gold progression depends on revelation/integration and heavy core use makes that harder.
- Rank changes lifespan and the strategic/social relationship between people of different ranks. Diamond is ageless and should remain exceptional.

ATE action: calibrate serious, active adventurers toward Bronze in roughly 2–3 years after obtaining a complete ability set when they have sustained suitable work/training, while allowing weak training, interrupted careers, missing abilities, safety, poverty, geography, and life choices to stall people. Do not simply grant Bronze at a timer.

## Adventure Society

- The Society is principally a monster-response institution and issues notices/contracts based on known threat information.
- Membership has both power rank and a separate judgment/trust dimension; contract eligibility is not reducible to combat rank.
- Contract authority is scoped: members can receive temporary investigative/arrest or other authority from the commissioning government/institution rather than possessing blanket police powers.
- Serious failures can trigger inquiries/audits from higher Society authority. Inquiries can examine branch leadership, expedition decisions, corruption, legal compliance and member conduct, then produce demotions, loss of standing/membership and institutional reform.
- During extraordinary monster pressure the Society can enter emergency/surge operating rules.

ATE action: add member standing/judgment, contract authority, incident severity, inquiry cases, branch audits, findings and consequences. Failed missions, anomalous casualty clusters, missing teams, suspected corruption/restricted magic and major public harm should be able to initiate inquiries.

## Monster ecology

- Monster surges are periodic world-scale increases in magical saturation, not isolated random attacks. They increase monster manifestation and also essence/Awakening Stone manifestation.
- Monster response therefore changes labor markets, travel, adventurer demand, prices, government behavior and Society operations before/during/after a surge.
- Monster rank and higher-rank suppression should matter to threat assessment and response composition.

ATE action: mature ambient magic into surge cycles with forecasts/uncertainty, preparation, emergency rules, post-surge recovery and historical memory.

## Restricted magic and records

- Some essence/confluence paths are institutionally restricted because their powers predictably threaten innocents or incentivize destructive behavior.
- Registration/assessment can include magical examination and checking against restricted configurations.
- Institutions may buy dangerous essences out of circulation and may issue lethal contracts against sufficiently dangerous restricted users.

ATE action: create an endogenous restricted-practice registry based on observed harms and institutional history, with legal variation by polity/branch rather than a universal arbitrary blacklist. Preserve due process, corruption, disagreement and mistakes as simulation possibilities.

## Magic Society / knowledge continuity

- Magical civilization preserves structured knowledge of essences, combinations and manifestations. Magical records can update and circulate rather than every generation rediscovering basics.
- Knowledge organizations, trade organizations and training institutions develop methodologies for advancement and specialist practice.

ATE action: make magical knowledge an explicit durable resource: combination records, training methods, restricted-practice evidence, recipes, monster dossiers, maps, surge records and institutional archives can be discovered, copied, lost, censored, inherited and improved.

## Gods and cosmology

- Gods are active actors rather than distant belief objects and can communicate, grant resources and intervene.
- Gods and greater transcendent entities occupy different metaphysical categories with rules/authority constraining what they can do.
- Outworlders and dimensional incidents fit into a larger cosmological continuity rather than being isolated special cases.

ATE action: keep divine/transcendent actors bound to explicit domains, authority and intervention rules. Cosmological events must leave institutional, religious and historical consequences rather than functioning as one-off plot devices.

## Economy and everyday magic

- Quintessence is a widely useful magical input for ritual, alchemy and forging even though it cannot substitute for an essence.
- Craftspeople and noncombat essence users participate in advancement/economy differently from adventurers.
- Rank distribution varies by region because training quality, magic density and opportunity vary.

ATE action: preserve regional magical economies and training centers. Avoid one homogeneous global rank curve.

## Continuity principle

The key lesson is not any individual lore fact. HWFWM repeatedly lets one rule propagate through society: advancement creates training economies; monsters create the Adventure Society; Society failures create audits; dangerous powers create regulation; long lifespans change institutions; surges alter economics and logistics; gods being objectively real changes religion; dimensional rules create politics at cosmic scale.

ATE should apply the same continuity standard: every metaphysical rule needs downstream demographic, economic, legal, institutional, cultural and historical consequences.
