# Stage 0.5 Game-Ready Authority

## Immutable starting point

Production work in this branch starts from PR #5 exactly:

- branch: `sim/canonical-magic-progression`
- commit: `fbfbe58ef1e18fa35eb4d63af98cd66e00f64c4f`
- PR #5 itself is not modified.
- PRs #6-#12 are research evidence only. No implementation is authoritative merely because it appeared there.

This document records the accepted Stage 0.5 behavior that the new implementation must satisfy. When an older calibration note conflicts with a later explicit project-owner decision below, the later decision wins.

## Stage boundary

Stage 0.5 finishes the existing objective simulation and freezes a trustworthy pre-subjective baseline. It does **not** prematurely implement Stage 1 Psyche, Observation, Memory, directional subjective relationships, language, authored cosmology, or later material/health/combat architectures.

Later-stage design documents constrain interfaces so Stage 0.5 does not paint the project into a corner; they are not permission to create duplicate authorities now.

## Architectural rules

1. One authoritative owner per fact.
2. Build causes once; let many downstream systems use them.
3. Objective events and provenance remain inspectable.
4. No quota/controller maintains desired population, magic participation, or rank counts.
5. No broad annual "magic access" subsystem may independently own resources, markets, institutions, threat response, and personal choice.
6. Prefer sparse indexes, active sets, queues, cached projections, and event-driven transitions over repeated global scans.
7. The canonical 1,000-year simulation remains <=120 seconds with meaningful headroom preferred.
8. Optimization may not change causal semantics merely to improve timing.

## Canonical magic invariants

- A complete path is exactly three base essences plus the confluence created after the third.
- Each of the four essences has exactly five abilities: 20 abilities total.
- A person with 0-19 abilities is Unranked/body rank 0.
- Iron and above require a complete 20/20 path.
- Body rank is the minimum rank of all twenty abilities.
- Civilian occupation imposes no rank ceiling.
- Cores may accelerate raw development but do not substitute for understanding/integration.
- Gold requires legitimate whole-path mastery evidence; Stage 0.5 must not fake later subjective-cognition systems.
- Diamond remains exceptional.

## Mature-world initial condition

Year 0 is the start of the observed simulation window, not the invention of magic or civilization.

A mature connected magical society may begin with roughly 75-80% of eligible adults possessing at least one **actually absorbed** essence. This is a one-time initial-condition model, not a prevalence target.

Founders must use the same authoritative state as live history: physical/provenanced resources, absorptions, confluence/path state, abilities and valid rank state. The initial cross-section may include partial paths, complete paths, real ranked practitioners, teachers, Society continuity, inventories and opening institutional finance. Pre-observation state must be labeled as such rather than presented as newly invented year-zero history.

After initialization there is no corrective pressure restoring any percentage.

## Accepted mature rank behavior

Do not tune toward an old rank pyramid merely because an earlier note expected Iron to be largest.

A large Silver shelf is valid and expected when advancement naturally stalls there. The key health signals are:

- continuous new Iron and Bronze replenishment;
- real individual Silver -> Gold progress;
- relatively few Gold;
- exceptional Diamond;
- no prerequisite bypasses.

The last accepted research calibration separated upper-tier integration pressure:

- Silver -> Gold: 0.16 integration coefficient
- Gold -> Diamond: 0.08 integration coefficient

This calibration is frozen unless a correctness defect is demonstrated.

Reference research behavior at year 1,000 (diagnostic, never a quota): 143 Iron / 181 Bronze / 294 Silver / 12 Gold / 1 Diamond; 631 complete living paths; 809 living essence users; about 82% adult essence-user participation. The Silver population showed substantial partial Gold progress.

## Civilian access and markets

Magic is ordinary infrastructure in this setting, not a tiny adventurer caste.

- Ordinary employment/wealth must provide a viable route to first essence and path completion.
- A person must not need to be Iron before they can afford becoming Iron.
- Common/uncommon resources can circulate through discovery, markets, institutions, orders, commissions, travel and trade.
- Purchases are limited by real stock and affordability, not arbitrary annual purchase caps.
- A Magic Society order moves an existing resource; it never conjures the exact requested resource.
- Remote ordering may occur without first exhausting local stock. The current design expectation is approximately two weeks of delivery time represented as transaction provenance, not simulated daily travel.
- Rare resources may remain expensive/logistically difficult.

## Adventure Society training

The Adventure Society is allowed to operate a real cadet pipeline.

- Local conditions determine cohort capacity; there is no global target count.
- New cohorts can enter every year.
- Cadets pursue three base essences, confluence and 20/20 abilities.
- Reaching a complete Iron path is graduation.
- Incomplete cadets are not formal Adventure Society members and do not spam ordinary membership applications.
- Society provisioning uses actual resources, actual custody, actual finance and real institutional reserves.
- Society reserves are institution-wide where the institution can actually transport/order resources; branches are operational locations, not isolated universes.
- Once graduated, advancement is unrestricted by cadet status.
- Specialized/martial/adventuring-family schools may improve real training opportunity without granting rank.

## Resource ownership

- Physical magical-resource creation belongs to ecology/threat/resource authorities.
- Ownership, custody, consumption and provenance belong to resource/material authorities.
- Payment/change/liquidity belong to economy/currency.
- Society training, sponsorship and contracts belong to institutions.
- Threat/danger/opportunity belongs to threat ecology.
- Individual attempts remain in current Stage 0.5 person/Agency behavior.
- Rank prerequisites/evidence belong to advancement/progression.

Society expeditions may respond to actual institutional demand, but should not create demand-free warehouses or tailor exact resources to an individual.

## Currency

Ranked currency is conserved except at explicit canonical production/consumption sources.

Exact-value denomination exchange requires a real counterparty with the actual change. Valuation alone does not create change. Low-denomination liquidity must be solved through real monetary participants, production and circulation rather than free conversion.

## Validation funnel

At year 0 / 100 / 500 / 1,000 report at least:

1. eligible adults;
2. adults with >=1 absorbed essence;
3. 1 / 2 / 3 base essences;
4. confluence;
5. abilities awakened;
6. 20/20 complete paths;
7. Iron/Bronze/Silver/Gold/Diamond;
8. partial upper-rank ability progress;
9. attrition/deaths by stage;
10. ordinary and ranked-currency liquidity;
11. resource creation, stock, consumption, sinks and custody;
12. local/remote market and institutional fulfillment;
13. cadet admission/graduation;
14. threat pressure and response;
15. civilian vs adventurer progression;
16. provenance/institutional continuity;
17. population/species/settlement health;
18. state/event/resource/material growth;
19. runtime.

Diagnose the first causal bottleneck. Do not compensate for an earlier broken stage by tuning a later one.

## Explicitly deferred

Do not implement in Stage 0.5:

- persistent Psyche/subjective Memory/Observation/Belief v2;
- directional subjective relationship architecture;
- full language ecology/dialogue;
- full material-economy migration beyond fixes required for the existing simulation;
- full health/disease/pregnancy/body-condition architecture;
- focused injury/combat architecture;
- objective cosmology/resurrection redesign;
- player-facing systems.

The existing objective simulation must be clean enough that these later systems can attach without replacing duplicated truth.
