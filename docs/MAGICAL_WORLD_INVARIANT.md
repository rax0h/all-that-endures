# Magical World Invariant

ATE simulates a magical world, not a mundane world with a rare magical career layered on top.

- Magical reality, resources, ecology, institutions, trade, work, family life, and settlement history exist independently of whether an individual currently wants a magical path.
- A person's aspiration governs pursuit, selectivity, use, specialization, and willingness to pay. It does not govern whether magic exists or whether civilization can expose them to it.
- Common and uncommon essences are ordinary obtainable goods in mature connected societies. A working person can normally save for them. Local absence can be solved through institutional ordering when conserved stock exists elsewhere.
- Rare and especially legendary essences/stones can remain expensive, geographically constrained, or genuinely unavailable. Specific exceptional combinations may justify long searches.
- Adventure Society sponsorship may provision suitable common/uncommon essences rapidly, including before a recruit is ranked. Requiring Iron before provisioning would be circular.
- Civilian paths are first-class: farmers, chefs, smiths, crafters, builders, architects, healers, merchants, scholars, and others can pursue magic for their profession without being combatants.
- Resource conservation remains authoritative. Markets, orders, and sponsorship move existing resources; they do not create them.
- Three base essences still form the confluence normally. Exact canonical 20/20 completion remains mandatory before Iron rank.
- For mature, institutionally connected societies, at least 75% of eligible/adult people entering an essence path is the Stage 0.5 calibration expectation, with 75-80% as the initial normal band. This is a diagnostic expectation, not a quota, cap, or forced assignment. Causal cultures/regions may fall below it and healthy worlds may exceed it.
- "Magic user" for prevalence diagnostics means a living eligible person who has absorbed at least one essence. Full 20/20 completion and rank distributions are measured separately.

Cosmological cause remains intentionally unspecified until the cosmology phase.
