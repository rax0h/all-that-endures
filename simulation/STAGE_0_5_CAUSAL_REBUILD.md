# Stage 0.5 causal rebuild — isolated research line

Base: Astra PR #5 head `fbfbe58ef1e18fa35eb4d63af98cd66e00f64c4f`.

This branch is deliberately isolated from `main` and from archived PRs #6–#11. It is an experiment, not a continuation of those implementations.

## Goal

Preserve PR #5's canonical progression, evidence, currency conservation, provenance, determinism and <=120 second millennium architecture while fixing the *world causes* that leave magical civilization too thin.

The simulation is a magical world, not a mundane world with a magic-access controller bolted onto it. The mature-world observation is that at least roughly 75% of eligible/adult people should commonly have absorbed at least one essence in a connected magical civilization. That is an outcome diagnostic, never a quota or assignment rule.

## Hard boundaries

- Do not weaken 3 base essences + confluence, 5 abilities per essence, 20/20 ranked-path prerequisites, per-ability advancement, Gold understanding, or Diamond exceptionality to hit population numbers.
- Do not add a second annual person-by-resource simulation layer.
- Do not create exact desired resources for aspirants. Ecology creates physical resources; civilization discovers, owns, transports, trades, sponsors and consumes them.
- Do not make magic rare merely to make high rank rare. Basic/common magical participation and exceptional mastery are separate questions.
- Do not tune a global desire coefficient toward a target percentage. Real changing conditions may change a person's demand: profession, family/social exposure, institutions, danger, local magical ecology and actual opportunities.
- Do not implement Stage 1 personhood substitutes (fake memory/belief/self-concept counters) inside Stage 0.5.
- Every new hot-path operation must be bounded/indexed. No repeated scans of all historical events/resources/items per person.

## Causal ownership

Use existing owners instead of adding umbrella systems:

- `ambient_magic` / `magic_resources`: physical magical ecology, provenance, ownership and consumption.
- `magical_civilization`: aggregate extraction/circulation/institutional feedback from actual settlement conditions.
- `institutions`: Society branches, sponsorship/contracts/records.
- `currency` / economy: conserved payment and denomination mechanics.
- `agency`: individual action selection and actual ability use.
- `advancement` / `magic_progression`: prerequisites and evidence; never population calibration.
- `threat_ecology`: objective magical danger/opportunity and physical consequences.

## First hypothesis

PR #5 already has substantial resource abundance and a civilizational feedback loop. The first experiment therefore targets stale/broken feedback rather than increasing raw spawn rates:

1. Aspirations formed once currently remain effectively frozen even when a person's family, neighbors, occupation, institutions and danger change over decades.
2. Organized field recovery gives civilian demand almost no extraction capacity and can stall when there are no existing practitioners/adventurer aspirants.
3. Person-to-person circulation exits entirely when a settlement has no current practitioners, even if it has holders and new aspirants.

Repair those feedback paths *inside the existing magical-civilization owner*. Measure before adding anything else.

## Acceptance discipline

After each coherent change: focused tests -> full suite/smoke -> deterministic medium history -> canonical seed 843000 millennium. Preserve <=120 seconds. Inspect at minimum adult population, >=1 essence prevalence, 1/2/3 essence funnel, 20/20 paths, rank pyramid, resource production/available/consumed, purchases/transfers, Society activity, currency conservation, chronology violations and digest stability/explanation.

If the first hypothesis does not materially improve the funnel, diagnose the next causal bottleneck. Do not stack compensating mechanisms on top of a failed hypothesis.
